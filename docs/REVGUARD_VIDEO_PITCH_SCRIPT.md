```
SHOT 06: OPERATOR REVIEW QUEUE & BLOCKED CASES
================================================================================
```
* **Display / Visual Direction:**
  * **Screen:** Operator Review Intercept Queue (`/review`)
  * **Visual Actions:**
    1. Show the list of intercepted cases flagged with amber accents.
    2. Hover over the **Escalation Trigger** badges:
       - *High Transaction Amount (> ₹10,000)*
       - *Low AI Diagnosis Confidence (< 85%)*
       - *Excessive Retries (≥ 3 attempts)*
    3. Contrast this with **BLOCKED** cases: explain that blocked cases are hard-stopped due to explicit opt-outs (`STOP`), already settled payments, or terminated mandates.
    4. Click **`✓ APPROVE RECOVERY`** on an intercepted case to demonstrate human-in-the-loop oversight and show the immediate outreach dispatch.
* **Duration:** 35 seconds (`3:35 – 4:10`)

**Spoken Script:**
> *"Financial systems cannot rely on blind automation. That's why we created the **Operator Review Intercept Queue**.*
>
> *Cases are automatically routed here whenever risk thresholds are triggered: if the amount exceeds ₹10,000, if retry attempts reach three, or if AI confidence dips below 85%. Operators can inspect the full telemetry and either approve recovery with a custom phone override, or reject and suppress the case.*
>
> *Meanwhile, cases marked **BLOCKED** are permanently prevented from reaching customers. If a customer replies 'STOP', or if the mandate is already settled or cancelled, our deterministic safety interlock immediately locks the record—ensuring zero spam and full regulatory compliance."*
